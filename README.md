# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyzes sales performance, profitability, regional trends, product performance, discount impact, and monthly sales patterns using the Sample Superstore dataset.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data every day, but raw data alone does not provide actionable insights. This project transforms retail sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that enables decision-makers to monitor performance, identify profitable opportunities, and detect areas requiring improvement.

Using Python for exploratory data analysis (EDA) and Tableau Public for visualization, this project uncovers key business insights through interactive dashboards and data storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The objective of this project was to:

- Analyze overall business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Identify the most and least profitable product categories
- Evaluate regional sales and profitability
- Discover high-performing and loss-making sub-categories
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends
- Investigate the impact of discounts on profitability
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### Technology Drives Profitability
Technology generated the highest profit while maintaining a strong profit margin despite relatively lower average discounts.

### Furniture Reduces Overall Profit
Furniture recorded high sales but the lowest profit margin. Losses were primarily driven by the Tables and Bookcases sub-categories.

### West Region Leads Performance
The West region achieved the highest sales and profit, while the Central region recorded the weakest profitability despite healthy sales.

### Seasonal Trends
Sales peaked in November, while profits reached their highest level in December, indicating stronger year-end profitability.

### Discounts Do Not Guarantee Higher Profit
Higher discounts did not consistently result in higher profits, suggesting that discount strategies should be reviewed.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in Technology products due to their strong profitability.
- Review pricing and discount strategies for Furniture products.
- Investigate operational challenges affecting profitability in the Central region.
- Expand marketing efforts in the West region to maximize revenue.
- Reassess discount policies for low-performing products before offering promotions.
